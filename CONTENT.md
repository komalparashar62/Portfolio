## Product management expertise

**THE PRODUCT PERSPECTIVE**

A designer's eye. A PM's perspective.

My work as a Product Manager shapes how I design: understand the problem, make deliberate choices and stay connected to delivery.

### Find the right problem

Connect the business goal with what users need. Turn open questions into a clear brief and success criteria.

### Choose what matters first

Define the MVP, prioritise features and make scope decisions that fit the available time and resources.

### Bring everyone onto the same page

Translate ideas into requirements, user journeys and acceptance criteria so stakeholders and developers share the same understanding.

### Carry the thinking through delivery

Collaborate on feasibility, review implementation and define what to measure after launch.

## Working approach

**HOW WE'LL WORK TOGETHER**

A clear process. Room to explore.

Each project is different. We'll shape the scope together, with clear decisions and useful outputs along the way.

1. **Understand** — Your goals, your audience and the problem worth solving. Output: A shared brief.
2. **Shape** — The structure, key journeys and priorities for a useful first version. Output: A clear direction.
3. **Design** — Visual exploration, detailed interfaces and interactive prototypes. Output: A tangible experience.
4. **Deliver** — Organised handoff, developer collaboration and implementation review. Output: A considered launch.
